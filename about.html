About Me

Engineering practical software with intuitive human-centered design.

I am a 7th-semester Informatics Engineering student at Universitas Airlangga, dedicated to building software solutions where solid technical logic meets thoughtful, clean usability.

My technical focus spans full-stack web architecture, cross-platform mobile engineering with Flutter, and intuitive UI/UX design systems. I enjoy turning ambiguous real-world problems into clear, structured, and resilient digital products. Through hands-on development and real-world project deployments, I have engineered systems ranging from real-time customer ticketing platforms to multi-stage geospatial survey engines—always prioritizing code reliability, performance, and clean human interaction.

Beyond the terminal, I believe the best engineering is rooted in articulate communication, empathy, and continuous learning. As a freelance analytical writer on Kompasiana, I explore the societal and ethical dimensions of technology and policy. Simultaneously, my time in student governance as HR Development Staff at HIMTI UNAIR has given me practical experience in team leadership, talent mentoring, and cross-functional coordination.
