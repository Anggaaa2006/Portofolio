Reflections

Thoughts on my learning journey at Universitas Airlangga

My Learning Journey

Bridging Code, Design, and Leadership My portfolio is more than a collection of projects; it's a testament to the belief that the best digital solutions sit at the intersection of technical mastery, thoughtful design, and effective execution. This is the core philosophy I have cultivated throughout my academic and organizational journey

1. The Interplay of Code and Design As an Informatic Engineering student, I quickly realized that strong code is only half the battle. My interest in UI/UX Design and Visual Communication stems from the need to translate complex logic—such as the Backward Chaining mechanism in the InHealth project—into a simple, intuitive user experience. My work on interfaces, from the Blibli Concept to the InHealth Chatbot, taught me how to apply Information Architecture principles to guide users seamlessly, proving that a beautiful system must also be a usable one.

2. From Logic to Leadership My journey extends beyond the screen. My roles as a Logistics Project Coordinator and an HR Development Staff were instrumental in sharpening my soft skills. I learned that team leadership requires the same structured, problem-solving approach as debugging a program. Managing budgets, coordinating logistics, and designing comprehensive training programs developed my ability to handle complex operational challenges and ensure projects are delivered effectively, on time, and collaboratively.

3. The Power of Perspective My experience as a Freelance Writer for Kompasiana adds a unique dimension. The rigor of research-based writing on complex topics, like my analysis on BPJS waivers, is directly applicable to my engineering career: it enhances my analytical skills, forces clarity in communication, and helps me articulate the 'why' behind every technical decision.

Looking Forward

My commitment is to continuous improvement. I am passionate about mastering technologies like Flutter and JavaScript, and eager to leverage my multidisciplinary background—combining coding, design, and leadership—to tackle real-world problems and create impactful, user-centered digital products.
